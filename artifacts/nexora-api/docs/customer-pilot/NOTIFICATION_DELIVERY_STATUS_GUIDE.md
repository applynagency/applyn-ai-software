# Notification Delivery Status — Customer Guide

This guide explains what you see in the Customer Pilot portal when notifications are sent, delayed, or suppressed.

## Where to check status

- **Timeline** (`/customer-pilot/timeline`) — delivery outcomes in plain language
- **Readiness** (`/customer-pilot/readiness`) — operational status badges:
  - Notifications operational
  - Approval reminders operational
  - Support monitoring operational
- **Preferences** (`/customer-pilot/preferences`) — control which notifications you receive

## What the statuses mean

| What you see | What it means |
|--------------|---------------|
| Notification delivered successfully | The message reached your inbox in the portal |
| Notification delivery failed | A temporary issue occurred; your approval status is unchanged |
| Notification delivery is being retried | We are retrying automatically |
| Notification suppressed by your preferences | You opted out of this channel or category |
| Reminder delivery may be delayed | A temporary platform delay; check the portal directly for approval deadlines |

## Your approval is authoritative

Notification delays do **not**:

- Change your approval status
- Extend or shorten your approval window
- Trigger execution or rollback

Always check the **Approval** page for your current approval state and expiry time.

## If reminders are delayed

1. Sign in to Customer Pilot
2. Open **Approval** to review any pending decision
3. Note the expiry time shown on the page
4. Update **Preferences** if you want in-app reminders enabled

## Contacting support

If you need help, ask your operator to generate a redacted support bundle. They will never share secrets, tokens, or internal system details with you.
